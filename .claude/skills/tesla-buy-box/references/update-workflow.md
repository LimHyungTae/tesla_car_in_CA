# Current inventory and report workflow

## Evidence collection

Use the current date and America/Los_Angeles timestamps. Search the official Tesla used Model Y inventory around ZIP 94404 and a 200-mile radius with no artificial price floor. A reliable inventory tracker may be used to discover candidates, HW4, Days on Lot, price changes, and cached fields, but Tesla's order page and checkout remain final.

For every plausible candidate capture:

- full VIN and availability timestamp;
- year, exact trim, HW version, seating, wheels, exterior/interior colors;
- listing price, mileage, location, Days on Lot, price history;
- CPO status, FSD purchase status, included options and VIN-specific trials;
- shown Transport fee and whether direct pickup is possible;
- AutoCheck score, accident/damage, title, odometer, owners, liens, and prior use;
- Battery Health/BMS evidence, or explicit `Pending` if unavailable;
- original-delivery/warranty expiration when actually available.

Open AutoCheck for every candidate that could reach BUY/FAIR and for the most relevant WAIT candidates. A tracker `CLEAN` label is not a substitute. AutoCheck also cannot prove there was no unreported repair.

## Current money checks

Re-check all of these on every dated refresh because they change:

- Tesla used financing representative APR/down payment/term and whether it is an approval or illustration;
- Tesla Current Offers and explicit used/CPO exclusions;
- VIN-specific trials, Supercharging, connectivity, or software benefits;
- PG&E pre-owned EV rebate eligibility and complete-application deadlines;
- California programs such as DCAP/CC4A and whether new applications are open;
- federal used-EV credit status;
- Foster City tax rate and checkout fees when they may have changed.

Prefer Tesla, government, and utility primary sources. Cite claims next to the text they support. Do not net a rebate from OTD before approval, and do not treat a service trial as cash value or a financing promotion.

## PG&E timing

The 2026 Standard rebate deadline concerns receipt of a complete application, not merely the purchase date. Delivery and registration documents can take time. Treat the rebate as a tie-breaker and $0 in base OTD until approved; never rush into a failed Buy Box solely to chase $1,000.

## Report shape

1. State confirmed BUY count, conditional BUY count, and the recommended action.
2. Show the Buy Box and OTD transport ceilings.
3. Present priority WATCH vehicles with exact target prices.
4. Present exclusions with one decisive reason each.
5. Explain all pending checks, especially SOH.
6. Give concise financing/incentive changes.
7. Include source timestamps, formulas, and clickable official listing links.

Do not preserve yesterday's ranking language when a vehicle has sold or the decision rules have changed. A sold vehicle may remain only as a clearly labeled historical price anchor.
